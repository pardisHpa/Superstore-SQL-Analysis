## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes 9,800 retail transactions from the Superstore Sales dataset (2015–2018)
using SQL queries in SQLite Online. The goal is to extract actionable business insights
across regions, product categories, customer segments, and time periods.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

| # | Query | Business Question |

| 1 | Data Preview | What does the data look like? |

| 5 | Top 10 Products | Which products generate the most sales? |

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- 📍 **West region** leads in total sales ($710K) driven by order volume
- 💻 **Technology** is the highest revenue category ($827K) despite fewest orders
- 📦 **Canon imageCLASS Copier** generated $61,600 from just 5 orders
- 📈 **Revenue grew 50%** from 2015 ($479K) to 2018 ($722K)
- 🏠 **Home Office** customers have the highest average order value ($243)
- 🏆 **Sean Miller** is the top customer with $23,669 in total purchases
- 🚚 **60% of orders** use Standard Class shipping regardless of order size
- 📅 **November and December** are peak months driven by holiday demand
- ❄️ **February** is the weakest month — ideal for targeted promotions
